How to read these numbers

Sales (90 days)

The total value of orders in the last 90 days, including shipping and sales tax, as eBay reports it.

Listing views

How many times buyers opened the account's listings. More views with steady orders means the listings are working.

Percentage change

eBay compares the last 31 days with the 31 days before. A new account starts from a small base, so the percentage can look very large.
