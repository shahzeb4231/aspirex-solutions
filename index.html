eBay Business Course

Aspirex Solutions · Professional Training & Business Collaboration Program

Course duration3 months
Total course feePKR 30,000
Admission paymentPKR 15,000

Course overview

A structured three-month eBay Business Course designed to take learners from beginner to advanced level. The training focuses on the knowledge and practical skills needed to understand, build and manage an eBay business.

Training format

  • In-person classes at our office
  • Beginner-to-advanced learning pathway
  • Practical business guidance and mentoring
  • Guidance related to international marketplaces, including the UK, USA, Germany and Australia

Course fee & payment plan

Total course fee: PKR 30,000. An initial payment of PKR 15,000 is due immediately upon admission to confirm enrolment. The remaining PKR 15,000 may be paid in either of these ways:

Option A: Monthly instalmentsPKR 5,000 per month for 3 months (total remaining balance: PKR 15,000).
Option B: One-time paymentPay the remaining PKR 15,000 in one payment.

Important: The first PKR 15,000 must be paid at the time of admission. The instalment plan applies only to the remaining balance.

After-course opportunities

After completing the training, learners who are ready for practical work may have the opportunity to collaborate with Aspirex Solutions. They may work with our team using their own eligible business accounts, or may be considered for suitable account-management work with Aspirex Solutions.

Depending on business needs, suitability and performance, a separate working arrangement may be discussed. Compensation may be percentage-based (commission) or a fixed salary. All such arrangements are subject to a separate written agreement and are not guaranteed as part of course admission.

Important clarifications

The course fee covers training only. Any post-training work, account access, revenue share, salary, responsibilities or business partnership is separate from course admission and is not guaranteed. Such arrangements depend on suitability, performance, availability, eBay policies and a separate mutual agreement. Learners must use accurate account and business information and follow all applicable eBay rules.
